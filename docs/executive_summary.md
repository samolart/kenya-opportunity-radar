# Kenya Industry Opportunity Radar: one-page summary

**Question.** If a Clients & Markets team could focus business-development effort on a few industries in FY27, which ones, and how sure can we be?

**Data (all real, official).** KNBS Quarterly GDP Report Q1 2026 (real output by activity); CBK Bank Supervision Annual Report 2025 (bank loans and non-performing loans by sector); CBK Credit Officer Survey, June 2026 (demand for credit by sector); KNBS Economic Survey 2026 (sector indicators).

**Method.** Ten industry groups built from KNBS activities and CBK bank sectors. Five pillars, each scaled 0 to 100: size (2025 value added), recent growth (Q1 2026), track record (2021-25 CAGR), credit demand (banks reporting higher demand), credit health (inverse of NPL ratio). Weighted into a score, tested under five weighting scenarios.

| Rank | Industry group | Score | Rank range across scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Financial Services | 77 | 1 to 1 |
| 2 | Consumer & Retail | 63 | 2 to 4 |
| 3 | Public Sector & Development | 61 | 2 to 3 |
| 4 | Energy & Resources | 45 | 4 to 7 |
| 5 | Agribusiness | 44 | 2 to 7 |
| 6 | Real Estate & Construction | 42 | 5 to 7 |
| 7 | Technology, Media & Telecom | 34 | 5 to 9 |
| 8 | Health | 32 | 6 to 10 |
| 9 | Manufacturing | 30 | 6 to 9 |
| 10 | Transport & Logistics | 23 | 8 to 10 |

**Findings.**
1. Financial Services ranks first under every weighting: 6.3% growth in Q1 2026 and the lowest bad-loan ratio (3.3%) against 16.0% for the whole banking sector.
2. Trade has the strongest business credit demand (65% of banks see it rising), but 19.9% of trade loans are non-performing: growth and risk travel together.
3. Real Estate & Construction is building fast (cement +20.3%, construction output +9.4% in 2025) but has the highest stress (24.1% NPLs; 29.5% for building and construction).
4. Agribusiness is the largest sector (18.3% of GDP) yet slow (3.1% in 2025) and its rank is the most sensitive to weights.

**Deliverables.** Excel model with live formulas and a 161-line reconciliation to Python; interactive dashboard with adjustable weights; ten industry briefs with client conversation starters; draft thought-leadership article; four-week content calendar with post copy.

**Limits.** Industry groups are my own grouping, not an official taxonomy. Public Sector and Health have no matching bank sector, so their credit pillars are neutral (50). Transport and Communication is one CBK sector shared by two groups. The latest KNBS GDP is Q1 2026. The score ranks relative attention; it is not a forecast.
