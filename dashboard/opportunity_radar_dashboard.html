Method and limits

Ten industry groups are an analyst grouping of KNBS activities and CBK bank sectors (the mapping is editable in the Excel model). Each pillar is scaled 0 to 100 across the groups and weighted. Groups with no matching bank sector get a neutral 50 on the two credit pillars, so their rank rests on growth and size alone. Transport and Communication is a single CBK sector shared by Technology, Media & Telecom and Transport & Logistics. The score ranks relative attention, not forecasts. Latest KNBS GDP is Q1 2026; Q2 2026 was not yet published.

Sources: KNBS Quarterly GDP Report Q1 2026; CBK Bank Supervision Annual Report 2025 (Table 18); CBK Credit Officer Survey, quarter ended June 2026; KNBS Economic Survey 2026.
