| gdp_q1_2026.txt | KNBS, Quarterly Gross Domestic Product Report, First Quarter 2026 (June 2026) | Real GDP by activity, constant 2016 prices (Table 2) |
| annual_report_2025.txt | CBK, Bank Supervision Annual Report 2025 | Gross loans and NPLs by sector, Dec 2025 (Table 18) |
| cos_jun2026.txt | CBK, Commercial Banks Credit Officer Survey, quarter ended June 2026 | Credit demand, standards and NPL outlook by sector |
| economic_survey_2026.txt | KNBS, Economic Survey 2026 (popular version) | Annex 1 key indicators 2021-2025 |
Also reviewed but not used: CBK Statistical Bulletin Dec 2023 (different loan definition), KNBS 2026 Facts and Figures, CBK Credit Officer Survey March 2026 (the June report already contains the March column).
