                       Remarks from the
                       Director General



                       T
                              he Popular Version draws highlights from the comprehensive
                              dataset presented in the 2026 Economic Survey published by
                              the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS). It is designed
                              as a user-friendly summary, highlighting key economic
                       developments and indicators for the year 2025.

                       The Economic Survey is the Bureau’s flagship publication, that provides
                       an overview of socio-economic trends over the period 2021 to 2025. It
                       serves as a critical resource for informing, budgeting, planning, moni-
                       toring, and policy formulation, while also guiding national development
                       priorities and investment decisions.

                       The statistics presented in the report are compiled from multiple sourc-
                          es, including KNBS surveys, censuses and administrative data
                             generated by Ministries, Departments, County Governments,
                                and various establishments..
